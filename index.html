Gait marks

Steps and turns on a portion of a bout. Open a …-PhonePack.zip from Files (AirDropped from the Mac). The frames stay on this phone.

Marks are saved on this phone as you go. When a portion is done: ⋯ → Export, AirDrop the CSV to the Mac, then Import phone marks in the annotator. Delete the zip afterwards: the frames identify the walker.
